Bloqade is [QuEra Computing](https://quera.com)'s software development kit (SDK) for
neutral atom quantum computers — a hub of embedded domain-specific languages (eDSLs) built on top of
[Kirin](https://github.com/QuEraComputing/kirin), the Kernel Intermediate Representation Infrastructure.

These guides walk you through installing Bloqade, understanding the physics behind neutral atom quantum
computing, and writing your first digital (gate-based) and analog programs.
